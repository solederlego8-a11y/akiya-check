20 Japanese listing words that should slow you down

Japanese listings rarely hide the bad news — they print it, in kanji most foreign buyers scroll past. Screenshot this sheet and check every listing against it before you fall in love with the price. Free, sent to your inbox.

What's inside

STOP3 deal-breakers — 再建築不可 (rebuilding prohibited), 告知事項 (disclosure matters), 土砂災害特別警戒区域 (landslide special warning zone). Do not proceed until each is explained in writing.
CHECK12 price- and permission-changers — including 接道義務 (road frontage duty), セットバック (setback), 市街化調整区域 (urbanization control area), 浄化槽 (septic tank), 借地権 (leasehold) and 旧耐震 (old seismic standard). Ask before paying a deposit.
NOTE5 know-before-you-buy words — including プロパン (propane gas), 井戸 (well water), 旗竿地 (flag-shaped lot), 居住義務 (residency obligation) and 用途地域 (land-use zone). Not a problem by itself, but know it before you buy.
20Every word comes with its reading, English meaning and severity tag, so you can scan any listing on SUUMO or AtHome yourself.
