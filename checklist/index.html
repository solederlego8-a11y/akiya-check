The 12-point akiya risk checklist

Twelve questions that catch most of the problems hidden in cheap Japanese houses — each with the Japanese phrase to paste into an email to the agent. Free, sent to your inbox.

What's inside

1Is the property 再建築不可 (rebuilding prohibited)?
2Does the lot touch a 4 m road for at least 2 m (接道義務)?
3Is it inside a 市街化調整区域 (urbanization control area)?
4What does the official hazard map say about flood depth here?
5Landslide warning zone (土砂災害警戒区域) — yes or no?
6Public sewer or septic tank (浄化槽)? Last inspection date?
7–12Water supply · gas · leasehold · disclosure matters · akiya-bank conditions · pre-1981 seismic standard — with the exact Japanese wording, in the email version.
