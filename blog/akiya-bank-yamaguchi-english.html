Akiya bank in Yamaguchi: how to search it in English, relocation subsidies and hazard checks (2026)

Short answer: Yamaguchi's prefectural akiya portal sends house-hunters to a gateway of municipal banks, Sunde minai ne! Butchiee Yamaguchi, and to the national At Home and LIFULL HOME'S banks. The content is in Japanese, so search with browser translation and the words 空き家バンク, 売買 and 賃貸; the prefecture's portal header does carry an "Other Languages" link. Vacant houses are common (19.4 percent of the stock in 2023). Relocation money has two tiers, and the smaller one reaches beyond Tokyo. The hazard Yamaguchi is best known for is landslide and debris flow, as in Hofu in July 2009.

Where Yamaguchi's akiya listings are

The prefectural portal. The Housing Division's akiya portal (083-933-3883) lists consultation windows, seminars and free consultation sessions, links to housing subsidy searches, reuse videos, information on home inspection and warranty insurance, and akiya bank information. A page of akiya bank contact windows gives the department and phone number for each municipality that runs a bank.

The municipal gateway. Sunde minai ne! Butchiee Yamaguchi is run by the prefecture's Regional Development Promotion Division. Its akiya bank page asks you to click the municipality you want, groups the municipalities into areas (Iwakuni, Yanai, Shunan, Yamaguchi and Hofu, Ube and Sanyo-Onoda, Shimonoseki, Nagato and Hagi), and links to the At Home and LIFULL HOME'S akiya banks. The prefecture's page puts the number of municipalities with bank contact windows at 19.

The scale. The prefecture's vacancy page reports from the 2023 Housing and Land Survey that Yamaguchi had 140,700 vacant houses, a vacancy rate of 19.4 percent, ranking 8th among prefectures and up from 17.6 percent in 2013, against 13.8 percent nationally. A high vacancy rate means choice, but also many houses nobody has looked at in years.

Whichever site you use, the listing is an introduction and the conditions are on the municipality's page: see our akiya bank conditions guide.

Relocation money: two tiers

The prefecture's relocation support page (updated 21 April 2026) describes two categories. Category A pays ¥1,000,000 for a household of two or more and ¥600,000 for a single person, plus ¥1,000,000 per child under 18. It requires five or more of the previous ten years in the Tokyo 23 wards or the non-disadvantaged Tokyo area, and at least one continuous year immediately before the move in those areas, with commuting to the 23 wards as an employee or sole proprietor where relevant (the national framework is on the Cabinet Office page). Category B pays ¥500,000 for a household and ¥300,000 for a single person, plus ¥500,000 per child, and its origin list is wider: the Tokyo area, Aichi, Kyoto, Osaka, Hyogo, Hiroshima or Fukuoka, with the same five-of-ten-years and one-year logic. Employment through the Yamaguchi Job Navi (20 hours a week or more, permanent), a five-year intention to stay, and an application within a year of moving are required. The page lists contact departments for the municipalities.

The page did not mention nationality or residence status, and a buyer arriving from overseas would not meet either origin list as written. Ask the destination town in writing. For renovation money, the portal links to housing subsidy search sites run by the Yamaguchi housing promotion councils; amounts depend on the municipality.

Hazards specific to Yamaguchi

Landslide zones

The prefecture's landslide zone designation page lists 25,725 landslide warning zones and 23,842 special warning zones (as of 5 June 2026) and says that designation across the whole prefecture was completed in December 2016. Check an address on the prefecture's landslide portal map, then read the hazard map guide and, for a slope lot, the retaining wall guide. A lot outside any zone is not guaranteed safe.

July 2009: Hofu

The Cabinet Office's white paper account of the July 2009 heavy rain in the Chugoku and northern Kyushu regions records, for Hofu, 72.5 mm of rain in one hour and 275.0 mm in 24 hours. It says debris flows killed 22 people in Hofu, at a special nursing home and near National Route 262 among other places, and that the disaster as a whole left 35 dead and 59 injured. For a valley or hillside akiya, ask whether the house or lot was affected by past rain, where the water goes in a downpour, and who maintains any drainage, culvert or retaining wall (see also the inspection guide).

Typhoons

The Japan Meteorological Agency's 1991 to 2020 normals count 3.0 typhoon approaches a year for the Chugoku region, against 5.8 for the Japanese mainland (JMA). Treat roof, windows and drainage as normal inspection items.

Three things buyers miss in Yamaguchi

  • The gateway is a directory. Conditions and subsidies sit with each town.
  • The smaller relocation tier is still origin-based. Category B reaches Osaka, Aichi and others, but not an overseas address.
  • Zones are many. With tens of thousands of designated zones, the map for the exact lot is the check that matters.

Local items to check in Yamaguchi

  • Land title. Inheritance registration and boundary survey.
  • Farmland. Akiya with farmland.
  • Rebuild permission. Saikenchiku-fuka and urbanisation control areas.
  • Septic tank. Running costs.
  • Real price. How to check sold prices.

Checklist for a Yamaguchi akiya

  • Use the gateway to open the town's own akiya bank page and read the conditions.
  • Ask in writing whether foreign nationals can use the bank and any subsidy.
  • Check which relocation tier, if any, your origin fits.
  • Check the landslide zone map for the exact lot and the slope above it.
  • Ask about past rain damage, drainage and any retaining wall.
  • Ask about build year, foundation and renovation needs (pre-1981 houses).
  • Arrange a building inspection before you commit.

FAQ

Where do I search for akiya in Yamaguchi?

The prefecture's akiya portal links to a gateway run by its Regional Development Promotion Division, Sunde minai ne! Butchiee Yamaguchi (ymg-uji.jp/house/bank/), which lists the municipal akiya banks by area and also links to the At Home and LIFULL HOME'S national banks. The pages are in Japanese, although the prefecture's portal header has an Other Languages link.

How common are vacant houses in Yamaguchi?

The prefecture's page on the 2023 Housing and Land Survey reports 140,700 vacant houses, a vacancy rate of 19.4 percent, ranking 8th among prefectures and up from 17.6 percent in 2013, against 13.8 percent nationally.

Does Yamaguchi have a relocation support fund?

The prefecture's page, updated 21 April 2026, lists two categories. Category A, for people who lived in or commuted to Tokyo's 23 wards, pays ¥1,000,000 for a household and ¥600,000 for a single person. Category B, for people from the Tokyo area, Aichi, Kyoto, Osaka, Hyogo, Hiroshima or Fukuoka, pays ¥500,000 and ¥300,000. The page reviewed did not mention nationality.

What happened in Hofu in July 2009?

The Cabinet Office white paper says Hofu recorded 72.5 mm of rain in one hour and 275.0 mm in 24 hours, and that debris flows killed 22 people in Hofu, including at a special nursing home and near National Route 262. The disaster as a whole left 35 dead and 59 injured.
