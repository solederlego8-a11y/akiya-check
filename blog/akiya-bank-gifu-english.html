Akiya bank in Gifu: cheap houses, English search tips, Hida snow and landslide checks (2026)

Short answer: No English-language Gifu akiya portal could be confirmed. Gifu Prefecture publishes a Japanese-language list (FY2026 edition) that links to each municipality's own akiya bank, and the prefectural page groups 39 municipalities across five regions: Gifu, Seino, Chuno, Tono and Hida. The prefecture's page gives no eligibility rules, so whether a foreign buyer can register is a question for each town. "Cheap houses in Gifu" therefore means browser translation and reading each town's own conditions. Before a low price excites you, check three things at the exact address: landslide zoning (the prefecture counts roughly 10,600 steep-slope warning zones alone), winter snow if the house is in the mountains, and rebuilding rights. Rules and prices change, so confirm with the municipality.

Where Gifu's akiya listings are

1. The prefectural list. The prefecture's vacant-house measures page (updated 24 July 2026 at the time of reading) links a PDF, "Akiya bank system status by municipality" (FY2026 edition), and lists municipality links by region: Gifu 6, Seino 11, Chuno 13, Tono 5, Hida 4. Clicking a municipality name opens its own akiya bank site. The same page links a PDF of municipal support programs and a PDF of municipal contact desks. The page does not say how many of Gifu's municipalities lack a bank, and it gives no eligibility rules.

2. Municipal banks. Each bank sets its own registration steps, fees and conditions. Some are run by the municipality, others by a non-profit or a local organisation. Our guide to akiya bank conditions and the residency requirement guide explain what typically appears. Search the Japanese term 岐阜県 [town name] 空き家バンク.

3. National banks. LIFULL HOME'S and At Home aggregate listings nationwide. Use them to see which Gifu towns have stock, then go to the town's own page. Neighbouring prefectures follow a similar pattern: see the Nagano and Niigata guides.

What the official data says

Landslide: Gifu is mostly mountains

The prefecture's steep-slope countermeasures page says about 10,600 warning zones for steep-slope collapse were designated as of 31 March 2025. That counts one of three hazard types (the others are debris flows and landslides), so it is not a total across all of them. The prefecture's 砂防課 (erosion-control) page describes two designations: a warning zone (yellow), and a special warning zone (red) where building structure is regulated and certain development needs prefectural permission. It also says agents must disclose zone status in a sale. For a lot-level check, use the prefecture's hazard-point map and the national Hazard Map Portal (MLIT and the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan). Our hazard map walkthrough explains the colours, and slope lots raise retaining-wall questions (retaining wall guide). A lot outside a zone today is not proof of safety.

Flood and snow: no prefecture-wide figure used here

Flood depth varies street by street, especially near rivers and in valley bottoms, and no single prefecture-wide figure that could be verified is given here. Check each address's depth and duration layers on the Hazard Map Portal. For snow, the Hida area (Takayama, Hida, Gero and Shirakawa-mura) is the part of Gifu most associated with winter conditions, but we could not read the official designation list of heavy snow areas for this guide. Ask the municipality whether the exact address is in a designated heavy snow or special heavy snow area. Our guide to buying in heavy snow areas lists the questions to send.

Three things buyers miss in Gifu

  1. Landslide-zone lots. With thousands of designated zones, a valley or hillside house may sit inside one. Ask for the zone drawing and read what the red zone restricts before relying on a renovation plan.
  2. Rebuilding rights and zoning. A cheap rural house may be on land where rebuilding is restricted, or in an urbanisation control area; see rebuilding-prohibited properties and urbanisation control areas. Houses with farmland can also need a permit (farmland guide).
  3. Winter and utilities in the hills. Roof snow, road clearing, heating fuel (propane vs city gas), wells (well water) and septic tanks (jokaso guide) rarely show in summer photos.

Costs and prices

No reliable Gifu-wide average price was found in official sources for this guide, so none is given. To see what comparable houses sold for in a given town, use MLIT's Real Estate Information Library, explained in our sold-price guide. Budget separately for the agent fee, taxes (overview), an inspection (inspection costs), renovation (estimates) and, in the mountains, a winter reserve. Municipal support programs are listed in the prefecture's PDF; amounts and conditions vary and many carry residency strings, so treat any subsidy as unconfirmed until the town confirms it in writing. See zero-yen akiya costs for why a "free" house rarely is.

Checklist before you contact a Gifu akiya bank

  • Find the town on the prefecture's FY2026 list and note its Japanese name.
  • Ask who operates the bank and whether foreign residents may register.
  • Check the exact address on the hazard maps and ask for the landslide zone drawing.
  • Ask whether the address is in a heavy snow area and who clears roof and road.
  • Ask about water source, septic tank records and heating fuel.
  • Confirm road access and rebuilding rights in the registry and zoning (registry guide).
  • Ask whether any renovation or purchase subsidy is open, and its conditions.
  • Book an inspection before paying a deposit.

FAQ

Is there an English version of Gifu's akiya bank?

Not that could be confirmed. The prefecture publishes a Japanese list that links to each municipality's own akiya bank. Browser translation works for reading; confirm conditions with the municipality in writing.

Can a foreigner use a Gifu akiya bank?

The prefectural page checked for this guide gives no eligibility rules, so nothing can be assumed either way. Conditions are set by each municipality or operator; ask in writing before registering.

How cheap are houses in Gifu?

No reliable prefecture-wide price figure was found in official sources for this guide. Check comparable sold prices in MLIT's Real Estate Information Library for the town, and budget separately for repairs, winter costs and taxes.
